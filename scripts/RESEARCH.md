
### Round LXXVII, addendum — the formal tree of a Haar-random 3-adic root is critical

Exact computation (classes mod `3^12`, valuations to `30`): the depth-`j`
mass at exponent `1` of the formal tree of a root class, `Z_j`, has Haar
mean exactly `1` at every depth (Theorem A as a finite identity), second
moment `1.35, 1.62, 1.90, 2.19, 2.49, 2.79, 3.09, 3.39, 3.70, 4.01` for
`j = 1 … 10` (linear, slope `0.30`), minimum over classes `0.24 … 0.039`
(decay `≈ j^(−0.8)`), and the fraction of classes with `Z_j < 0.1` rising
`0, 0, 0.019, 0.025, 0.049, 0.047, 0.048, 0.052, 0.055, 0.058`.  So the
exponent-`1` formal mass is critical in the branching-process sense (mean
`1`, variance growing linearly, driven by rare deep-descending lineages),
there is no `L²` bound and hence no cheap "generic fatness" theorem for
3-adic roots, and the thin roots that the min over lifts optimises against
form a small but slowly growing set, not a null set.  Recorded as a
measurement; nothing here bears on the integer tree of `1`.
