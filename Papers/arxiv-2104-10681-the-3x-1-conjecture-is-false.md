# arxiv-2104-10681-the-3x-1-conjecture-is-false

Citation: Robert Tremblay, "The 3x+1 Conjecture is false", arXiv:2104.10681 [2021].

Authors: Robert Tremblay

arXiv: [2104.10681](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.10681)

Year: 2021

Classification: structural

Abstract:

We will prove that there are trajectories generated by the functions at the origin of the 3x+1 and 5x+1 problems which are divergent. The iterative application of these functions on the set of positive integers allows us to determine that nearly 17% of all these integers, in the 5x+1 problem, belong to divergent trajectories. Regarding the 3x+1 problem, this percentage becomes very small, suggesting that all positive integers are part of converging trajectories. Despite this appearance, we will prove that this percentage will never be zero and that there will be an infinity of integers belonging to divergent trajectories, thus leading to the conclusion that the 3x+1 conjecture is false.
