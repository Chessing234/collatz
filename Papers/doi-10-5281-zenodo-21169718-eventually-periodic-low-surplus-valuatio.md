Abstract:

We study infinite valuation chains for the accelerated Syracuse map via their exact finite-word residue classes and least positive odd representatives. Each finite valuation word determines a unique realization class modulo a power of \(2\), and compatible prefixes therefore define a natural \(2\)-adic inverse-limit object. We prove that an infinite chain is realized by a positive odd integer if and only if its least representatives eventually stabilize; in particular, infinitely many strict jumps exclude positive odd realization.
