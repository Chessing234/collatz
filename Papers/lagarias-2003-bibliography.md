# lagarias-2003-bibliography

Citation: Jeffrey C. Lagarias, "The 3x+1 problem: An annotated bibliography (1963--1999) (sorted by author)", arXiv:math/0309224 [math.NT].

Source checked: <https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0309224>.

arXiv metadata: submitted 13 Sep 2003; last revised 11 Jan 2011 as version 13; subjects Number Theory (math.NT) and Dynamical Systems (math.DS).

Abstract statement recorded: the 3x+1 problem concerns iteration of the map on integers given by `T(n) = (3n+1)/2` if `n` is odd and `T(n) = n/2` if `n` is even. The 3x+1 conjecture asserts that for every positive integer `n > 1`, the forward orbit of `n` under iteration by `T` includes `1`.

Lean file: `Collatz/Papers/Lagarias2003Bibliography.lean`.

Formalization status: statement recorded as a proposition; several finite computations are checked. No proof of the conjecture is claimed.
