Abstract:

The Collatz Conjecture (3x+ 1) has long resisted proofs based on continuous symmetries and traditional combinatorial models due to its chaotic, discrete nature. In this paper, we map the conjecture into a problem of Non-Abelian Topological Dynamics using the Topological Chromodynamics (TCD) and Dynamic Mass Transduction (DMT) frameworks. We redefine the integer sequence as the thermodynamic evolution of a topological defect subject to a metric friction tensor (Ξµν) and Kardar-Parisi-Zhang (KPZ) non-linear pumping. We demonstrate that the universal 4 → 2 → 1 loop represents the fundamental quantum ground state (zero-point energy) of the discrete vacuum lattice.
