Abstract:

The Collatz Conjecture (the 3n+1 problem) is one of the most famous open problems in mathematics, stating that any positive integer eventually reaches the repeating cycle 4 -> 2 -> 1. Despite supercomputing sweeps testing trillions of trajectories, classical mathematics lacks a formal proof due to the assumption of an unbounded linear number line anchored at absolute zero (0). This paper presents a theoretical resolution by re-grounding number space in the Epsilon Framework under the primary axiom of a non-zero baseline plenum (bar{exists} 0, ε > 0) within a closed, nested toroidal topology.
