# arxiv-2101-04067-collatz-cycles-and-3n-c-cycles

Citation: Darrell Cox et al., "Collatz Cycles and $3n+c$ Cycles", arXiv:2101.04067 [2021].

Authors: Darrell Cox, Sourangshu Ghosh, Eldar Sultanow

arXiv: [2101.04067](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.04067)

Year: 2021

Classification: cycle

Abstract:

Halbeisen and Hungerbuhler determined optimal bounds for the length of rational Collatz cycles. Their methods are extended to $3n+c$ cycles. Another sequence having properties similar to those of Riemann zeta function zeros is introduced.
