Abstract:

We reformulate the Collatz conjecture within a structural space I term the "Mirror," omitting even integers as reflections of odd ones. We index the odd domain strictly by their first step in the original map, naturally yielding the natural progression {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, ...}. This Mirror space, topologically equivalent to the classical system, provides a clear perspective unveiling its hidden geometry and systems.
