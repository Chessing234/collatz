For backstepped first-passage shells, we prove short-time localization, derive an exact inverse-affine representation of the fixed-time kernel, and reduce the shell-slice discrepancy to weighted primitive-frequency correlations. We also prove a quantitative boundary-layer estimate and identify a formal renewal model for the corresponding shell mechanism.
