Abstract:

The Syracuse Problem despite its simplistic 3n+1 nature, has remained unproven for decades. This paper introduces a new way to visualize the Collatz pattern by forming a 2-dimensional curved manifold (Collatz Manifold) bounded with 3 heterogeneous boundaries in a dim 3 non-homogeneous parity coordinate system, a modified Euclidean Space E with dim E = 3. The topology of the manifold is defined using boundary tangents and normal scaling with the entire surface.
