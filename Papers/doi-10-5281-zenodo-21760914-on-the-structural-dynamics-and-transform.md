This paper explores the structural behavior of the Generalized Collatz Function (GCF)parameterized by a scaling factor K ∈ R+. For K = 3, the GCF naturally maps to theclassical 3n + 1 Collatz problem. We formally investigate the tree-like topology generatedby this mapping across different intervals of K. We prove that for 1 < K < 2, the statetrajectories are strictly monotonically decreasing, ensuring that the induced graph structures are strictly finite trees.
