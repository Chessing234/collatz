Abstract:

Traditional definitions of natural numbers based on Peano axioms rely on "static picking," which, according to the Löwenheim–Skolem theorem, structurally allows for the existence of non-standard models (ghost numbers). Within this static framework, dynamic processes such as the Collatz conjecture are reduced to the halting problem, limiting research to the accumulation of empirical data. This paper proposes a fundamental shift from static picking to a "dynamic process definition" by integrating the concept of "waves as a cause of binary outcomes" (the causal determination process via superposition and interference) into the very definition of natural numbers.
