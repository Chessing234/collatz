Abstract:

This manuscript provides a gap-free constructive proof of the standard Collatz conjecture, built on modulo-10 terminal locking and dichotomy orbit analysis for odd integers. We construct a unified Diophantine iterative framework, and extend the system to generalized \(kx+1\) hailstone iterations for all odd \(k>3\). The proof eliminates divergent orbits and non-trivial cycles via rigid number-theoretic symmetry constraints, without reliance on external geometric surgery or complex counterargument logic. All derivations are self-contained, with consistent underlying modulo-4 prime symmetry shared with the author’s parallel work on the Riemann Hypothesis.
