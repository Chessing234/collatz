Abstract:

This work develops a structural framework for the Collatz dynamics based on a fine decomposition into local operations of the form \((3x+1)/2^\ell\), where \(\ell = v_2(3x+1)\) encodes the 2‑adic depth of each step. Each such “brick” induces a logarithmic height increment \(\alpha - \ell\) and corresponds to a geometric vector in a discrete set of angular directions. This angular viewpoint reveals two weak directions (\(\ell=1,2\)) and infinitely many strongly dissipative ones. Using this structure, the dynamics of any trajectory can be decomposed into complete elementary blocks, each consisting of a bounded neutral segment followed by a strictly dissipative segment.
