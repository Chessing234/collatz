Abstract:

The famous L. Collatz problem 3x+1 needs no introduction. This article used the method of proof by contradiction regarding the possible existence of cycles other than the 4–2–1 cycle. The work provides evidence in favor of the fact that closed numerical cycles in the Collatz sequence cannot exist. The results discussed are demonstrated using some examples.
