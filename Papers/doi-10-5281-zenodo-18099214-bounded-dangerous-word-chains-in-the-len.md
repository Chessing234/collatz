Abstract:

This record contains a preprint and a fully reproducible bundle of code, data, and figures for an empirical study of bounded dangerous block chains in the length sixty four accelerated Collatz map. The work builds an explicit successor graph for the most frequent low valuation blocks in tail data and then validates the resulting drainage picture with two adic depth scans inside exact cylinders. The key finding is that dangerous continuations are extremely sparse and appear depth limited in large consecutive two adic slices, with the few depth three seeds explicitly enumerated and shown to eject immediately under tracing and lift neighborhood tests. The bundle includes the manuscript, scripts, raw scan summaries, node and edge tables for the successor graph, and the plotted figures...
