Classification: structural

Abstract:

In this work, we continue the development of a symbolic dynamical formulation for the Collatz mapping. This formulation has been previously demonstrated to induce a ternary alphabet for characterizing the behavior of trajectories generated by this mapping. While the previous installation of this series focused on local and quasi-global behaviors, this work presents more quasi-global results that contribute to further understanding of the Collatz mapping.
