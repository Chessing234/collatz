Abstract:

We develop a fully algebraic and structural framework for the accelerated odd Collatz map, based on an exact encoding of finite orbit segments by valuation words and the associated Diophantine identities. We show that the inverse dynamics is locally rigid and that, at a global level, it necessarily organizes into affine fibers uniquely determined by the exact iterated inverse equation, without introducing any local degrees of freedom. Orbits are treated exclusively as finite algebraic objects, anchored at a prescribed terminal value, and are described equivalently by their initial or final state together with a finite word of $2$-adic valuations.
