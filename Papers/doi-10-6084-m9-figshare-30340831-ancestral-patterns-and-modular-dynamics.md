Abstract:

The 3n + 1 problem still stands unsolved and it was first nagged by Lothar Collatz in 1937. Great efforts were mounted by both amateur and professional math lovers and witnessed in this drama. We demonstrate in this paper an integrated paradigm where new conceptual things are combined with classical entries for exploration. Our paradigm integrates topological transport reasoning—the interaction of inverse structures and forward orbits—the use of Cayley-colored graph rewrites, and action of modular arithmetic on inverse-predecessor trees.In this connection, we produce more results related to the module structure of odd predecessors, and pay special attention to the subcase where n ≡ 2 (mod 3)...
