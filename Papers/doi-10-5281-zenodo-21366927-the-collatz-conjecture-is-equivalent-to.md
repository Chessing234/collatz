Abstract:

We prove that the Collatz conjecture is equivalent to the conjunction of two independent conditions: (i) the Akin conjecture for a=3a=3a=3 (every integer's Collatz parity sequence is eventually periodic) and (ii) the absence of nontrivial integer Collatz cycles. The bridge between these conditions is a symbolic lemma: eventually periodic binary sequences in the "bad set" (aˉ<log⁡23\bar{a} < \log_2 3aˉ<log23, corresponding to divergent orbits) are mapped to negative rationals by the Bernstein conjugacy Φ\PhiΦ, and therefore cannot correspond to positive integers.
