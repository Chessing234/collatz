Abstract:

This is a continuation of our paper "Reformulating Collatz Conjecture Without Numerical Concepts", which provides a way to replace all numerical calculations, applied in the formulation of this conjecture, by simple symbol processing rules. We provide a simple and intuitive proof that there is no limit for the length of possible sequences, composed of odd numbers generated by the Collatz algorithm.
