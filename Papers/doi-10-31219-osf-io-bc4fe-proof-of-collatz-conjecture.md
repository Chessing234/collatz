Abstract:

Proof of the Collatz Conjecture is provided using the representation of a positive integer 𝑛 via a term 𝑘_𝑖 of its Collatz sequence (𝑘_𝑖), integer 𝑐_𝑖 - the number of odd terms of the Collatz sequence having 𝑖 terms, and integer 𝑏_𝑖 having a 𝑐_𝑖-special 3-smooth representation: 𝑛= (2^𝑖×𝑘_𝑖−𝑏_𝑖 )/3^𝑐_𝑖. The odd terms k_a_j, j>=0, are considered. It is shown that lim(3^j/b_a_j)j→∞=0. Finally, it is proven that any Collatz sequence collapses to the trivial Collatz cycle.
