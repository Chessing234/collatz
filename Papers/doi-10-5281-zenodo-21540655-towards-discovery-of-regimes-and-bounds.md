This deposit collects three sequential manuscripts constituting a single research program on the Collatz conjecture. The three drafts document the development, correction, and progressive compression of a large collection of arithmetic, dynamical, Green-function, statistical-mechanical, symbolic, and discrete-gradient approaches into a common set of exact variables and a comparatively narrow final family of proof obligations.
