Abstract:

We present a reproducible computational framework for finite-range scans related to three classical problems in number theory: the Strong Goldbach conjecture, the Collatz conjecture, and the twin-prime conjecture. The framework uses deterministic algorithms, explicit range partitioning, atomic checkpoints, merge-time coverage validation, machine-readable summaries, and streaming statistical analysis. Runs were performed at bounds of 10,000,000, 20,000,000, and 50,000,000.
