Abstract:

The Collatz Conjecture is a longstanding problem in mathematics that posits that regardless of the starting positive integer, the sequence generated by repeatedly applying the iterative function will ultimately converge to 1. Despite its apparent simplicity, the conjecture has remained unsolved for decades, leaving many to wonder whether it is a fundamental property of mathematics or an illusion. This study explores the connection between the Collatz Conjecture and prime numbers, examining the frequency of prime numbers in Collatz sequences and their impact on the behavior and properties of the sequences.
