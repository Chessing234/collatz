Abstract:

This paper establishes a rigorous, non-heuristic structural bounding framework for hypothetical non-trivial closed orbits (cycles) of the \(3x+1\) dynamical system over the positive odd integers \(\mathbb{Z}^+_{\text{odd}}\). By coupling global transcendental conservation laws with local \(2\)-adic lattice topology, we map the explicit geometric constraints of the state-space.
